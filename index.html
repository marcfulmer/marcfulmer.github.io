Economics Job Market Candidate

I am a PhD candidate in economics at Michigan State University and am on the 2025‑2026 job market seeking nonacademic positions. My research focuses on health, labor, and public economics, with a current interest in social insurance programs for injured and disabled workers. In my job market paper, I use decomposition analyses and large-scale survey data to systematically explain the 43.6 percent decline in workers’ compensation receipt from 2003 through 2018. I am particularly interested in applying my econometric and mathematical skills to positions in consulting, industry, and nonprofit research institutions.
