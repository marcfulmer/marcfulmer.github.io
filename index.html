Welcome!

I am a PhD candidate in economics at Michigan State University, specializing in health, labor, and public economics. My current research focuses on the social insurance programs that support workers experiencing injury, illness, or disability. For instance, my job market paper shows that improvements to workplace safety explain most of the long-term decrease in recipiency rates of workers’ compensation benefits.
